Research

What we learn building environments that try to defeat frontier agents: what holds, what saturates, and the artifacts that fake difficulty along the way.

  1. Preprint, November 2026

    Fair Environments Saturate: Lessons from Thirty Verifiable Agentic Environments Built to Defeat Frontier Models

    Rohan Pandey, Gauntlet Vector Labs

    We built and probed 32 fully specified, deterministically graded agent environments across eleven capability axes, from estimation and reverse engineering to long-horizon repair and novel Lean theorem proving. No environment that met the fairness bar held GPT or Claude frontier agents: all but one came within a tenth of the reference solution. The only clean hold came from computational hardness.

    • A falsifiable conjecture: fair, compute-light tasks are codeable, so a tool-using frontier agent saturates them.
    • Random syndrome decoding holds both frontier models at about 0.26 of the reference, with nothing hidden.
    • A catalogue of artifacts that fake headroom, and of reward hacks we closed in our own graders.
